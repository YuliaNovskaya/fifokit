Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your FIFOKIT account and cloud data from:

FIFOKIT → Settings → Account & cloud sync → Delete account

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, request account deletion by emailing:

ops@fifokit.com

Please send the request from the Google email address used for your FIFOKIT account.

Deleting your account removes

  • Your FIFOKIT account
  • Cloud-saved rosters
  • Cloud settings
  • Saved financial goal and pay inputs

Data stored on your device

Local rosters and finance data stored on the device are not deleted when the cloud account is deleted.
